      How calculations work

      • Change over time = (new price - earlier price) / earlier price x 100.
      • Market comparison = (market price - cheapest price) / cheapest price x 100, using the latest verified price per market and the same unit only.
      • Review flag = a monthly change above the threshold, or a statistically unusual jump. It asks for a check; it does not claim a shortage or an error.
      • National CPI describes the average household basket. It is not the price of a single product in a single market.
